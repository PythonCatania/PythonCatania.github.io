# Privacy Policy

This policy describes how the personal data of visitors to **catania.python.it** is processed. It is provided under Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR") and Italian Legislative Decree 196/2003 ("Privacy Code"), as amended by Legislative Decree 101/2018.

---

## 1. Data controller

The data controller is **Salvatore Rapisarda**, as Founder of **Python Catania**.

Contact: **catania@python.it**

---

## 2. Types of data processed

### 2.1 Browsing data

This is a static website hosted on **GitHub Pages**, a service provided by GitHub, Inc. To serve the pages and keep them secure, GitHub may log technical data such as the visitor's IP address, the date and time of the request and the requested page. Python Catania has no access to these logs and does not use them. Their processing is described in the [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### 2.2 Data provided voluntarily

If you write to the email address shown on the website, your address and any other data in your message are used only to reply to your request.

### 2.3 Cookies and local storage

This website **does not use profiling, analytics or third-party cookies**.

The only data stored on your device is your choice about the cookie notice: when you click "Got it!", your browser saves the value `cookieconsent_status` in its local storage, so the notice is not shown again. This is a technical tool, it contains no personal data and does not require consent under Article 122 of the Privacy Code. You can delete it at any time from your browser settings.

### 2.4 Links to external services

The website links to third-party services (for example Meetup, Telegram, GitHub, YouTube and other social networks). When you open them, your data is processed under their own privacy policies. In particular, event registration takes place on the Meetup platform.

## 3. Purposes and legal basis

- **Running and securing the website**: legitimate interest of the controller (Art. 6.1.f GDPR).
- **Replying to user requests**: steps taken at the request of the data subject (Art. 6.1.b GDPR).

---

## 4. Transfers outside the EU

Python Catania does not sell, share or disclose personal data to third parties.

GitHub, Inc. is based in the United States. Browsing data is transferred to the United States under the safeguards described in GitHub's privacy statement, including its participation in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

---

## 5. Retention period

- **Browsing data**: as set by GitHub.
- **Email communications**: for as long as needed to handle the request, and no longer than 2 years.
- **Cookie notice choice**: until you delete it from your browser.

---

## 6. Your rights

You can exercise the rights set out in Articles 15–22 GDPR at any time:

- access to your personal data
- correction of inaccurate data
- erasure of your data
- restriction of processing
- data portability
- objection to processing

To exercise them, write to **catania@python.it**.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, the [Garante per la protezione dei dati personali](https://www.garanteprivacy.it).
